# CR-028 – Promote HJ-001 to Human Reviewed and Approved

| **Priority** | Low |
| **Affected Documents** | HJ-001 – Project Vision |

---

# 1. Background

HJ-001 – *Project Vision* has provided the strategic foundation for the HotJoes architecture throughout the definition of Epic 1.

The document has remained intentionally stable while the supporting architectural artefacts have matured through multiple iterations, independent reviews and architectural refinements. Those downstream artefacts now consistently reflect the business vision originally established by HJ-001.

The Project Vision has therefore completed its role as a draft proposal and has become the approved strategic baseline for the project.

This Change Request formally promotes HJ-001 from Draft to Approved without altering the business vision, project objectives or strategic direction.

During promotion, HJ-001 shall also be aligned with the current HotJoes Markdown presentation standard used by later architectural documents (for example HJ-003 onwards) where appropriate. These presentation changes are editorial only and shall not modify the document's meaning.

---

# 2. Objectives

This Change Request shall:

- promote HJ-001 from **Draft** to **Approved**;
- assign the next approved document version in accordance with the HotJoes versioning convention;
- record formal human architectural approval;
- update document metadata;
- align the Markdown presentation with the current HotJoes document standard where appropriate;
- update repository references that still identify HJ-001 as Draft; and
- preserve the existing business vision and strategic intent.

---

# 3. Scope

This Change Request applies only to:

- document metadata;
- revision history;
- document presentation;
- repository traceability.

It shall not modify:

- the Project Vision;
- Business Vision;
- Vision Statement;
- Business Objectives;
- Business Capability Roadmap;
- Business Principles;
- Success Criteria;
- Scope Boundaries; or
- any strategic business intent.

---

# 4. Required Changes

## 4.1 Document Metadata

Update the document metadata as follows:

- Version → next approved version in accordance with the project versioning convention.
- Status → **Approved**.
- Last Updated → approval date.
- Retain the existing Document ID, Title, Classification and Owner.

---

## 4.2 Revision History

Add a new revision history entry recording that:

- the document has completed human architectural review;
- no changes were made to the approved business vision;
- the document has been promoted from Draft to Approved.

The revision history shall clearly indicate that this is an approval milestone rather than a technical revision.

---

## 4.3 Markdown Presentation

Review HJ-001 against the current HotJoes Markdown presentation standard used by newer architectural documents (HJ-003 onwards).

Where appropriate, update only presentation aspects such as:

- heading consistency;
- table formatting;
- spacing;
- Markdown rendering consistency;
- document layout; and
- other editorial presentation improvements.

These changes shall be formatting only.

They shall not:

- change wording;
- alter section numbering;
- modify document structure;
- change business intent; or
- introduce new strategic content.

---

## 4.4 Related Document Status

Update the Related Documents table to reflect the current approved status of any documents that have themselves been formally approved.

No document shall have its status changed unless it has already completed its own approval process. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## 4.5 Repository References

Update repository references that still identify HJ-001 as **Draft** so they correctly identify the document as **Approved**.

Only status references shall be updated.

No architectural relationships or traceability references shall otherwise change.

---

# 5. Architectural Constraints

The promotion shall not:

- alter the Project Vision;
- modify the Vision Statement;
- change Business Objectives;
- change Business Principles;
- modify Business Capability Roadmap;
- change Scope Boundaries;
- introduce architectural guidance;
- introduce implementation guidance; or
- modify relationships with downstream architectural artefacts.

This Change Request is administrative and editorial only.

---

# 6. Expected Outcome

Following application of this Change Request:

- HJ-001 becomes the approved strategic vision for the HotJoes platform.
- The business vision remains unchanged.
- The document records formal human architectural approval.
- Document metadata reflects its approved status.
- Markdown presentation is aligned with the current HotJoes documentation standard.
- Repository references correctly identify HJ-001 as an approved document.
- HJ-001 continues to provide the stable strategic foundation from which the remaining architectural artefacts are derived.
